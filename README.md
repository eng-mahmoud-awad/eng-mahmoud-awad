### 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm a frontend developer with a design background — I don't just build interfaces, I design them first. That means fewer handoffs, faster iteration, and products that actually look the way they were meant to.

- 🎨 Started with graphic design — branding, typography, visual identity
- 💻 Moved into frontend development to bring my own designs to life
- ✨ Now I do both, end to end — from Figma file to deployed product
- 🌱 Currently deep into motion design and scroll-driven interactions
